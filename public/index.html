Beginner Cooking Guide

Simple Vegetable Rice Bowl

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1/2 cup chickpeas or beans
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh herbs
  • Lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  2. Add garlic and cook for about one minute.
  3. Add mixed vegetables and cook until tender.
  4. Add chickpeas or beans and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Serve over warm rice.
  6. Top with fresh herbs and lemon juice before serving.

Cooking Advice

Helpful Veg Food Cooking Tips

Use Colorful Vegetables

Add different colored vegetables to meals for better flavor, texture, and visual appeal.

Add Protein Sources

Include beans, lentils, chickpeas, tofu, paneer, nuts, seeds, or dairy depending on your diet.

Season Gradually

Taste as you cook and adjust salt, herbs, spices, lemon, or sauces slowly for balanced flavor.
